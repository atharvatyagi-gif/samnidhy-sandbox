Try the screen

How strict is the health check?

The screen keeps companies whose annual reports score well on nine yes/no health questions (the Piotroski F-Score). Move the slider to see how many of today's stocks would stay in at each threshold.

Counts are from the latest daily screen, among stocks with complete annual-report data. The site's own rule is the value shown under "Rule 1" on the Advanced page. This is a way to understand the screen, not a prediction.

A day in the lab

What refreshes, and when.

  1. 07:30 IST

    Morning update

    The day's data update runs first thing, so the page is ready before the market opens. A second pass follows at 09:00.

  2. 09:00 to 16:00

    Market hours

    The live-price job runs about every 5 minutes and re-ranks the ten on fresh prices. The price source delays quotes by about 15 minutes. News sentiment refreshes every 30 minutes.

  3. 19:00 IST

    After the close

    Once NSE publishes its official end-of-day files, the screen is re-run on closing prices and the session is saved to the archive.

  4. Overnight

    World and news

    World markets, the globe layers and news sentiment keep refreshing hourly.
