Questions people ask first

What is this site?

A free learning lab run by students at TAPMI. It turns real data from the National Stock Exchange of India into lessons, a daily stock screen and a research terminal, so you can see how markets behave instead of reading about them.

Should I invest based on what I see here?

No. Everything here is educational analysis by students, not investment advice, and the models are often wrong. Past performance does not predict future results. Talk to a SEBI-registered adviser before investing real money.

Where does the data come from, and how fresh is it?

Closing prices come from NSE's official end-of-day files. Prices during the day come from free public sources and run about 15 minutes behind the exchange. Every page shows when its data was last updated, and says so when something is late or missing instead of guessing.

What do "Simple view" and "Pro view" change?

Simple view explains each number in plain words and keeps the detail behind "Show details". Pro view drops the explanations and opens every detail at once. The data is identical; flip the switch at the top whenever you like, and the site remembers your choice on this device.

Who can use the terminal?

TAPMI members, with their institute email. The lesson and the screen are open to everyone.
