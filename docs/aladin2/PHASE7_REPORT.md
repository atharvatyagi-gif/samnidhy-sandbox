The full 15-point checklist, run and pasted as measured, is in `docs/aladin2/ACCEPTANCE.md` (regenerate with `python -m scripts.aladin2.acceptance`). **Result: 12 PASS, 3 PARTIAL, 0 NOT MET at the item level**, with two failures inside the PARTIAL items that matter and are stated below.

## What was done
- **Stress tests** (`tests/aladin2/test_stress.py`, 8 tests): a 25% overnight gap through an ATR stop fills at the gap open and the loss is accounted to the paisa (daily P&L compounds to the trade); the paper-portfolio drawdown brake halves new sizes after a crash; circuit-limit lock stretches delay an exit to the next fillable open; zero-volume suspensions block entry and exit; bad data (missing bars, no-volume stretch, a 6x wild print) never crashes features or strategies and signals stay binary; flat zero-range bars give finite volatility inputs; a crash year whose 80% coverage fell to 70% (the 2020 number) suspends the engine and turns a signal into NO EDGE; an extreme-volatility regime (160% a year) keeps forecasts and path simulation finite.
  **The stress tests found a real flaw, now fixed:** a circuit lock lasts several days, but the engine only recognised the first locked day (later one-price days open at the unchanged previous close, so the 2% move test passed them as tradable). A lock is now treated as continuing while the one-price days continue. I re-ran the pooled policy on the full data: every figure is identical to the digit (no trade in this sample touched a consecutive-lock stretch), so no reported number changed.
- **Methodology drawer and README**: one source, `data/config/aladin2_method.json`, shown as the new **Method** page of the ALADIN 2.0 tab and rendered into a README block by `scripts/make_aladin2_readme.py`; a test proves the README block equals the text word for word, that the disclaimer is the configured one, that no banned word appears, that the survivorship caveat and every "not measured" item are present, and that the numbers quoted (2020 coverage, calibration error, AUC range) match the measured reports. `scripts/check_banned.py` now also scans this file and the README block.
- **`.env.example`** gained `FRED_API_KEY` and an ALADIN 2.0 note. **`python -m scripts.aladin2.sources_status`** prints a startup table (local data and its age, reachability, whether the key is set, never its value); the nightly job prints it in the ingest step.
- **Accessibility pass** from a real-browser check (`tests/e2e/aladin2_check.py`): it found three charts without accessible labels and a scoreboard with no numbers table; both fixed. Now every chart has an accessible name, hover text (107 SVG titles on the desk pages), a numbers fallback, an "As of" line and the disclaimer; Tab to a market-map tile and Enter opens that stock.
- **Budgets measured** (this PC, Chromium): desk shows its watchlist in about 1.2 s (limit 2 s); a 6 KB stock shard loads in a median 200 ms at 150 ms round trip and 1.6 Mbit/s (limit 300 ms); only the three small files the open stock's brief needs are fetched at page start; added to `site/`: 3.1 MB.
